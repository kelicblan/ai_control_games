# Survival-first food reserve

User requirement: survival comes first; maintain a minimum food stock and prioritize finding food below that stock.

The reserve is derived from living NPC count and food-trip consumption, with at least two meals per person. Replenishment aims one meal per person above the minimum, capped by warehouse capacity. Carried food and active food trips prevent redundant dispatch, but only deposited food counts as warehouse stock. Healthy, sufficiently rested NPCs prioritize food over ordinary work, voluntary idling and protest; acute hunger with available food, injuries, exhaustion, threats and committed deliveries retain precedence. Existing food trips finish without weather/social reviews endlessly discarding progress. No food is created by the policy.

## Exact calculations and acceptance

Estimate trip seconds as twice the route via storage, gate (50,205), bridge exit (50,300), and the sixth-nearest seeded food patch, divided by 80% of movement speed, plus gathering time and a 20-second margin. Minimum is ceil(living * max(2, (difficulty hunger rate + .045) * tripSeconds / 44 + 1)), capped at 90. Target is minimum + living, capped at 95. Empty population yields zero. Pending meal commitments are deducted from projected stock. Carried food counts once; unladen living collectors contribute at most four units or remaining patch yield. Existing hunts count at most four units while their animal is alive. Dead workers and depleted patches contribute zero.

Dispatch below the minimum, or while ongoing replenishment deliveries/trips remain, until projected food covers the target. Ordinary work is reconsidered on the next simulation tick; active food trips, meals, aid and explicit manual movement are not continually restarted. At exactly the minimum with no ongoing replenishment there is no forced dispatch. Physical injury, health <=35 in village or <35 outside, energy <18 and immediate threats interrupt outdoor work; real carried supplies are still returned. These bounds retain the existing retreat mechanics.
