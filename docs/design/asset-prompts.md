# Art sources

All included raster assets were generated for this project using the built-in Image Gen tool, then copied without pixel edits. HUD text, buttons, meters, selection markers and weather particles are native code.

## concept.png
Production-quality full Chinese woodland survival gameplay UI for 今晚别挨饿 / CAMPMIND. Warm storybook painted miniature diorama, deep pine app frame, ivory typography, amber fire. Top resource HUD and mission band, left three-character crew rail with portraits and meters, large camp clearing map and bottom command dock. Three adult Chinese explorers: brave young man with amber scarf, cautious young woman with teal scarf, caring middle-aged bearded man with plum scarf. No marketing page or device mockup.

## camp.png
Standalone empty woodland map using concept style, wide landscape, no UI/text/people. Top-down three-quarter flat mossy clearing framed by fir trees. Log area at 20%/38%, berries at 78%/34%, stone campfire at 51%/54%, log store at 32%/68%, canvas tents lower right, winding paths and supply chest left, rocky creek at far right. Golden afternoon light and finely painted production details.

## characters.png
Three-column, two-row portrait/sprite atlas, 1536×1024. First row square bust portraits with amber/teal/plum backgrounds; second row full-body three-quarter sprites of matching adults, centered in equal cells. Warm storybook style, backpacks, consistent lighting, no text. Transparent background requested for sprites.
